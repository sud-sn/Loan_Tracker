# Privacy notice for borrowers (consent step)

Shown in the app when a borrower is added, read to the borrower in their language, and recorded with
who recorded it and when (PRD section 12). `{years}` is the retention setting (8 by default).

## English
We record your name, phone, address, Aadhaar number and documents only to give and manage your loan
and to contact you about payments. They are stored securely on the lender's own computer, are not
shared, and are kept for {years} years after your loan closes. You can ask to see, correct or remove
your details.
