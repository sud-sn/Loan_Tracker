# 0016 · Restructure, seizure and sale, release, top-up, cheques, insurance (Phase 3)

**Status:** accepted (owner's instruction, 6 Nov 2026). Holidays and Sundays are **not** skipped:
due dates fall on the calendar day, as the owner works.

| Topic | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Frequencies | Every 3 months, every 6 months and yearly, besides monthly / weekly / daily / single. A rate per month is ×3 / ×6 / ×12 for the period; a yearly rate ÷4 / ÷2 / ÷1. Due dates keep the day of the month. |
| Restructure | Every installment not yet paid is replaced (shown as "Rescheduled"). Unpaid principal is spread over the new installments; overdue interest and the interest of skipped installments (an EMI holiday) are spread evenly over them. Installments can be added and the rate changed from here on (later part payments use the new rate). The late fee must be collected or waived first. Recorded with the reason. |
| Seizure | The vehicle is marked seized (towing / yard charges can be added as a charge). The app only keeps the record; the agreement and the law must be followed. |
| Sale | Sale expenses become a charge. If the price covers what closing costs on the day (interest to the day, no foreclosure charge), the loan is closed and any surplus is given back to the borrower (shown on the daily log as received and given back). Otherwise the price is paid into the loan and the rest is still owed (write it off if needed). |
| Release and no-dues | When a loan is closed, record who received the originals and when; a no-dues certificate prints in English and Tamil, with the Form 35 / NOC line for a vehicle. |
| Top-up | A new loan can close an active loan of the same borrower: what closing costs on the day (interest to the day, no foreclosure charge) is taken out of the amount handed over, the old loan's security moves to the new loan, and the old loan shows the settlement. |
| Cheques | Post-dated cheques are listed with their status; marking one *bounced* adds the bounce charge (₹500 by default). |
| Security and insurance | Security details can be corrected later (renewed insurance, registration once issued). Vehicles whose insurance ends within 30 days (or has ended) are listed on the home page. |
