# 0007 · Rates, terms and dates

**Status:** accepted · PRD §4 and §16

- Periodic rate: monthly rate → weekly × 7/30, daily ÷ 30; yearly rate → monthly ÷ 12, weekly
  × 7/365, daily ÷ 365.
- Installments from the term: months with weekly repayment → ⌈months × 30 / 7⌉; months with daily
  repayment → months × 30; days with monthly repayment → ⌈days / 30⌉; or give the number of
  installments directly.
- Simple interest for days is one payment at maturity; with a monthly rate and a term in months,
  each month counts as 30 days (3 months at 2% = 6%).
- Monthly due dates keep the first due day; 29–31 become the last day of short months and come back
  afterwards.
- Loan numbers use the year of the loan date; receipt numbers use the year of the payment date.
