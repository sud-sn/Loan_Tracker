# 0004 · Early settlement figure

**Status:** accepted · PRD §5 left the interest-to-date rule open

Settlement on a date = all unpaid principal + unpaid interest of installments already due + the
current period's interest pro-rata to the days elapsed + unpaid late fee − interest already paid in
advance on later installments. Interest of later periods is waived (recorded as a settlement waiver
with the payment). The same rule applies to every method, so flat and fixed-amount loans do not
charge unearned future interest. Part-prepayment with schedule recompute stays in Phase 2.
